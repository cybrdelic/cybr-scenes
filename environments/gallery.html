Six environments.
New native renders.

Executed CPU ray/path tracing of the recovered scene geometry, with source changes, native radiance, diagnostic views and recorded commands. No image-generation model or image upscaling.

“Verified” means successful native execution, finite transport, valid dimensions and recorded geometry—not a claim of photorealistic convergence or measured physical accuracy. Earlier supplied images can differ in revision, camera, resolution and sampling. The cave, water and lava retain their documented approximations. This gallery is an inspection page, not an interactive 3D engine.
